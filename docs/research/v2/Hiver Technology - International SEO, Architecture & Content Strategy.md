**Version date:** 14 August 2026  

> **Legal note:** I am an AI, not a lawyer. This is a working strategic analysis, not legal advice. Obtain qualified trademark and domain counsel in the United States, Australia and the United Kingdom before relying on the brand or domain recommendation.

> **Baseline reality:** The public site is not a functioning international agency site that merely needs optimization. It is a partially built WordPress/Elementor template with a visible construction banner, placeholder-proof signals, 79 homepage `#` links and unverified service/product claims. A low-authority `.com.np` domain in this state will not realistically rank for US, Australian or UK head commercial terms within its first year. The first job is to remove risk, verify capability and build a credible acquisition foundation. [1] [2]

### Recommended path

**Provisionally select a distinctive, legally-cleared international brand on a gTLD, then 301-redirect the current `.com.np` site once the new site is production-ready.** Google documents that country-code domains are a strong signal of a site’s intended country, while a gTLD is not associated with one location.[3] A gTLD alone does not solve the established `Hiver` entity collision; a distinctive brand is the stronger long-term option.

### Domain-migration controls

1. Freeze the URL inventory, including root posts, `/insights/`, `/products/`, `/services/`, `/portfolio/`, `/team/` and `/about-us/`.
2. Map each legacy URL to its closest relevant new canonical URL with a one-hop 301; do not mass-redirect pages to the home page.
3. Launch the new canonical system, XML sitemap, internal links, verified analytics/consent and factual Organization identity.
4. Verify both properties in Search Console, use Change of Address if eligible, submit the sitemap, and monitor index coverage, redirects, queries and landing pages.
5. Update real directory, profile, partner and permitted client links.
6. Treat the recovery window as `DATA REQUIRED — real GSC baseline and migration evidence`; do not promise a recovery date.

## 13. Link Acquisition

The link plan is deliberately relationship-led: accurate Clutch/GoodFirms/DesignRush/LinkedIn profiles where eligible; ecosystem participation only with genuine capability; permissioned client/partner links; real Nepal tech-community coverage; founder thought leadership; and original data/process assets worth citing. Paid link networks, PBNs, mass guest posting, comment spam and low-quality directory blasts are prohibited. Link acquisition is the main gate on Tier B/C terms.

## 14. Roadmap and Honest Expectations

**Expectation setting:** In 3 months, the realistic outputs are a clean, credible, crawlable site, early indexation/impression data and possibly the first qualified enquiries if proof and distribution work exist. In 6 months, supported Tier-A/Tier-B content and directories may create demand. In 12 months, Hiver may have enough evidence to assess selected Tier-B expansion. No individual ranking, traffic or revenue outcome is promised.
