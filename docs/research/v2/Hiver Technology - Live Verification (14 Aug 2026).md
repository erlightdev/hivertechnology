## Brand-collision verification

### Hiver SaaS — https://hiverhq.com/

The public Hiver SaaS homepage is live and positions **Hiver** as an omnichannel customer-service platform for the agentic era. It prominently exposes Product, Customers, Resources, Pricing, Sign in, Book a demo and Start free trial. The page states **10,000+ teams trust Hiver**, shows recognizable customer-logo proof, and links a large product/resource/entity footprint including AI Agents, AI Copilot, AI Help Center, customer stories, integrations, developer docs, trust/security pages and company pages.

This is direct evidence that the single-word `Hiver` entity is already strongly associated with another software company. Hiver Technology should not use `Hiver` alone as a KPI, title strategy or primary navigational target. The site should consistently use `Hiver Technology`, `Hiver Technology Nepal`, or a new approved brand. A qualified trademark attorney should review the name in the US, Australia and UK before material investment; this is not a legal conclusion.

### Provisional recommendation

Do not recommend `hivertechnology.com` or `hiverhq.com` as available options. Treat `hiver.tech` as a technically plausible candidate only after trademark counsel reviews the Hiver collision and Hiver confirms the name is commercially safe. The stronger strategic recommendation is to evaluate a distinctive brand name rather than moving the same collided `Hiver` name to another TLD. If Hiver keeps the name, use `Hiver Technology` consistently and obtain qualified trademark advice before the domain migration.
